Putting information in: readings, meals and notes

Home is built so a reading, a meal or a note takes seconds. The big number at the top is your latest reading. Below it are quick forms for a glucose reading, for food, and a link to a diary note.

Go deeper: why type readings and notes yourself?

Sensors measure glucose. Only you know what you ate, how you felt, and what else was going on. When a doctor looks into low glucose, the questions are: what were the symptoms, what was the glucose at that moment, and did the symptoms ease once glucose came back up?[[endo-2009]] A note with a time on it answers the first and third, and puts them next to the second.

Finger-prick readings matter most. They are what both Abbott and Dexcom point you to when a sensor reading doesn't match how you feel.[[abbott-au-differences,dexcom-au-mismatch]] A reading you type here, or that comes in from the Bluetooth meter, appears on the graph as a diamond beside the sensor's line.

Back-dating. If you treated a low and forgot to log it, "When" lets you record it afterwards at the right time. A few rough words ("shaky, had juice") are far better than nothing.

Go deeper: what's worth a note

The Diary note link on Home saves free text with a time. Good things to record: how you felt, alcohol, exercise, illness, a missed or late meal, and any medicine your doctor has asked you to track. healthdirect lists extra activity, delayed or missed meals, alcohol (especially without food), and vomiting or diarrhoea among the things that can cause lows in people with diabetes,[[healthdirect-hypo]] and a consensus report notes alcohol can raise the risk of a delayed low.[[evert-2019]] You don't have to work out whether something mattered. Write it down and let the timeline show.

Go deeper: the device lights and the Sync dot

Each device you use gets a light under the Sync button:

  • Pulsing blue: syncing now.
  • Green: it worked in the last 12 hours.
  • Amber: it worked, but longer ago.
  • Red: the last try failed. Tap the light to see why.
  • Grey: not set up yet.

The dot in the header is simpler: green if anything synced in the last 6 hours, amber if not.
