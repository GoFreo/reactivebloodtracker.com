When something doesn't work

Most problems are one of a few things. Find yours below. For anything about a device itself, the maker's own support comes first.

The Dexcom line is hours behind the Dexcom app.
That's expected. Dexcom holds back readings for other apps by three hours outside the United States.[[dexcom-api-delay]] For anything happening now, use the Dexcom app or a finger-prick.
The Libre line is behind, or has gaps.
This app gets Libre readings through LibreLinkUp, so it can trail the Libre app. Tap Sync. A new sensor needs a one-hour warm-up before it gives readings.[[abbott-au-faq]] Grey "No data" bands on the graph mark stretches where the sensor had nothing to send.
Sync says the passcode is wrong.
The sync passcode belongs to whoever runs this copy of the app. Re-enter it in Settings. If you aren't sure what it is, ask the person who set the app up.
The meter isn't found, or the device list closes.
Switch the meter on just before you tap Sync, and keep it on until the Bluetooth symbol shows, because its Bluetooth only stays on for a short time. Use Chrome on a Mac, Windows PC or Android phone. Safari and Firefox can't do this.[[mdn-bcd-bluetooth]] A meter pairs with only one device at a time, so if it was paired with another app or phone, switch that Bluetooth off first.[[roche-guideme]] If nothing works, type the finger-prick in.
The sensor and my finger-prick don't match.
That's common, and it's why the graph draws both. See Sensor or finger-prick? Why they differ. If a reading doesn't fit how you feel, trust the finger-prick, and follow the maker's steps.[[abbott-au-differences,dexcom-au-mismatch]]
My records are gone.
Clearing browser data deletes them, and Safari clears a site's data after seven days without use unless the app is on the Home Screen.[[webkit-itp]] If you have a backup, restore it with Home → Export → Import. Next time, add the app to your Home Screen and back up now and then.
The numbers here differ from the device's own app.
This app receives data second-hand and can trail the device's own app. The device's own app is the one to use for anything happening now.
It keeps asking "what happened?"
Answer it, or tap Dismiss. You can raise the amount of change it asks about, or switch the question off, in Settings.
The video doesn't play.
It needs an internet connection, and it only appears when the video file can be reached. Check your connection and reopen Help.
