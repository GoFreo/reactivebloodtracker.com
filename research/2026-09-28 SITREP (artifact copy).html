Follow-up report

How the meal builder works

  1. Under the Food box, tap 🧺 Build a meal from ingredients.
  2. Type an ingredient, its grams, and the per 100 g carbs from the label. The item's carbs appear straight away. If you already know an item's carbs, type them in Carbs g.
  3. Or tap Barcode: the product drops in as a new row with its label figure; add the weight.
  4. Save as usual. Readings → Meals then shows "🧺 36 g carbs from 2 ingredients" alongside what your glucose did afterwards.

Worked example from the tests: 40 g rolled oats at 60 g/100 g = 24 g, 250 ml milk at 4.8 g/100 ml = 12 g, total 36 g.
